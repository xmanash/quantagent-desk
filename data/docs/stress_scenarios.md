# Historical Stress Scenario Catalogue

## 2008 Global Financial Crisis (2008_GFC)
Severe credit and equity crisis following Lehman Brothers collapse.
Typical moves observed:
- Equity indices (SPX): -35% to -45%
- USD 10Y yield: -100 to -200 bps
- Investment-grade CDS: +100 to +200 bps
- VIX: peak above 80

## COVID-19 March 2020 (COVID_2020)
Rapid liquidity-driven sell-off at the start of the pandemic.
- Equity: -30% to -35% in weeks
- Rates: flight-to-quality, yields collapsed
- Oil (WTI): extreme negative prices temporarily
- Volatility explosion

## 2022 Rates Shock (RATES_SPIKE_2022)
Aggressive Federal Reserve hiking cycle.
- USD 10Y: +200 to +300 bps from trough
- Equity correction ~20%
- Credit spreads modestly wider

These scenarios are used as base cases for hybrid generative stresses.
